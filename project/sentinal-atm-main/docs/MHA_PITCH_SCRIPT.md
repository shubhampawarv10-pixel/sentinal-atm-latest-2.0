# SentinelATM: MHA/I4C Winning Pitch Script & Presentation Guide
**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) | Problem Statement: SIH26184**

## ⏱️ The 3-Minute Judge Winning Script

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook (The National Emergency)
> "Respected judges, every single day, the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP) and the 1930 helpline receive over **8,000 cybercrime complaints**.
> 
> When an Indian citizen loses their life savings to a 'Digital Arrest' scam or a UPI phishing fraud, the money is bounced through 3 to 4 layers of mule bank accounts.
> 
> And within **15 to 45 minutes**, the scammers withdraw hard currency from local ATMs. By the time police receive the bank statements days later, the cash is gone.
> 
> Today, we present **SentinelATM** — India's first proactive cybercrime intelligence framework that **forecasts likely ATM cash withdrawal locations in advance** to catch scammers before the cash leaves the machine."

---

### [0:30 - 1:00] Direct Mapping to the 4 Key Deliverables
> "We built SentinelATM directly around the Ministry of Home Affairs' four core deliverables:
> 
> 1. **Component A: Predictive Analytics Engine:** Our multi-hop graph tracer identifies the terminal cash-out mule and calculates the transaction velocity score.
> 2. **Component B: GIS Risk Heatmap Dashboard:** An interactive geospatial radar that clusters real physical ATM nodes using OpenStreetMap and isolates the highest-risk ATMs.
> 3. **Component C: Law Enforcement Interface:** A dedicated dossier providing complete evidentiary trails for police investigators under Section 65B.
> 4. **Component D: Alert & Notification System:** Instant push dispatches to beat police patrol vans via Telegram/SMS, while triggering fund-blocking APIs with banks via CFCFRMS."

---

### [1:00 - 2:15] LIVE DEMO (The Stage Showstopper)
*(Switch screen to the SentinelATM Command Center)*

> 
> 1. Click on **'Deliverable A: Multi-Hop Mule Flow'**: Notice how the money bounced from Paytm Payments Bank $\rightarrow$ HDFC Bank $\rightarrow$ and landed in a Punjab National Bank terminal mule account in Badarpur. Transaction velocity is **84.5/100** — an emergency.
> 2. Now look at **'Deliverable B: GIS Risk Heatmap'**: The system immediately mapped all active ATMs within a 1.5 km corridor of the terminal mule anchor. 
> 3. Look at Rank #1: **State Bank of India (24x7 e-Corner) on Mathura Road** has a **68.4% match** with an intercept window of the next 18 to 25 minutes!
> 4. Watch this live on stage: I click **'Dispatch Beat Patrol'** — *(Hold up your phone)* — **My smartphone just received a live tactical police alert on Telegram with the exact ATM coordinates and turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation!**
> 5. Simultaneously, our **CFCFRMS Switch** triggers an automated debit freeze on the mule card."
